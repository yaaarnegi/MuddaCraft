Origin & Maker Hub

From Uttar Pradesh to Your Home

For generations, skilled craftspeople across rural Uttar Pradesh have harvested riverbank reeds and seasoned wild bamboo, hand-lacing them into resilient furniture.

Instead of routing through layered wholesale middlemen, regional traders, and expensive urban retail stores, we connect directly with small manufacturing workshops in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh.

This direct link enables honest, transparent pricing, ensures traditional structural binding techniques are preserved, and delivers authentic regional craftsmanship directly to your doorstep.

Honest Maker Promise: We operate on a direct-sourcing model. We do not make unsupported claims about environmental certifications or artisan wage multipliers — we simply provide genuine, time-tested Indian furniture directly from the makers to you.
