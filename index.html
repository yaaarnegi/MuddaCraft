Heritage & Technique

The Anatomy of an Authentic Mudda

For centuries, master weavers along the Yamuna and Ganges river basins have transformed seasonal wild reeds (*Saccharum bengalense*) into architectural seating that is featherlight yet incredibly sturdy.

01

Reaping & Sun-Curing

Wild sarkanda stems are harvested in late autumn and cured under the sun for 20 days until golden and resilient.

02

Bamboo Ring Framing

Fire-bent green bamboo bands form the dual structural rings at top and base to distribute load evenly.

03

Mandala Cord Weaving

Master artisans weave concentric cord geometric patterns that create springy, ergonomic cushion without foam.

Care & Product Information

How much weight can a Mudda stool support? ▾

Because of the vertical cylindrical orientation of the dense reed bundles, standard Mudda stools easily support between 120 kg to 150 kg (265 to 330 lbs). They are engineered naturally by the interlocking bamboo rims.

Can I keep my mudda outdoors on a patio or balcony? ▾

Yes! Muddas love covered verandas, balconies, and patios. For open outdoor areas, choose our Veranda Weatherproof Stool treated with natural linseed oil, and bring it inside during heavy rain.

How do I clean and maintain my mudda? ▾

Simply wipe with a dry or lightly damp cloth. You can also vacuum using a soft brush attachment. Keep in a dry, ventilated area.
