ORIGIN & MAKER HERITAGE

The Living Craft of Muzaffarnagar

Generations of seasonal Sarkanda reed harvesting, manual cane bending, and honest Indian furniture making direct from maker workshops.

Along the riverbanks and canal plains of western Uttar Pradesh, the seasonal Sarkanda reed (Saccharum bengalense) grows wild. For over a century, local craft communities in Muzaffarnagar have harvested these resilient tall stalks after the monsoon, peeling the golden outer layers and sun-drying the inner core until it gains its signature tensile flexibility.

A Legacy Built Without Screws or Glue

Unlike modern composite or factory-pressed furniture, a genuine Indian Mudda contains no nails, hidden metal fasteners, or chemical resin glues. Its strength is entirely structural: hundreds of tightly bundled vertical reeds form an hourglass or cylindrical core, locked into place by heavy tension twining of jute and natural cotton cords.

The top and bottom rims are bent from seasoned solid bamboo, cross-stitched with reinforced tire cord or heavy-gauge thread binding. This creates a piece of seating that weighs only 3 to 6 kg, yet comfortably supports over 120 kg with natural ergonomic flex.

Direct from the Workshop Floor

Traditionally, Mudda furniture has traveled through layered middlemen: local aggregators, regional wholesale mandis, transport brokers, and expensive urban retail stores. By the time a chair reaches a customer in Bengaluru or Mumbai, its price has doubled while the maker receives only a fraction.

MuddaCraft operates on a direct maker connection. We work hand-in-hand with A to Z Mudda House and family workshop clusters in Muzaffarnagar. We handle surface freight logistics and direct packaging, ensuring makers earn honest wages and customers receive genuine regional craft at fair, uninflated prices.

The Honest Maker Promise We do not invent fairy-tale sustainability certificates or artificial artisan wage multipliers. We simply deliver genuine, durable, time-tested Indian furniture straight from the hands that weave it to your veranda, balcony, or living room.
